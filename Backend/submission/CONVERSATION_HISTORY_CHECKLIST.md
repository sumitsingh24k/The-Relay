# How I worked with the assistant - evidence checklist

The assignment asks for real, unedited assistant conversation history or exported prompts.
Before submission, add the following actual evidence to this folder or your submission archive:

- Conversation/export showing initial requirements and implementation decisions.
- One prompt that shaped the RAG/safety design and why.
- One prompt that shaped experiments and why.
- One prompt that caught a mistake and why.
- One decision you overruled or made yourself.
- A short statement of what is distinctly your own work and reasoning.

Do not fabricate screenshots, history, or user feedback. This file is intentionally a checklist, not claimed evidence.
